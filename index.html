[ Gabriel I. Perren ]

I earned my Licentiate degree in Physics in 2008 and my PhD in Physics in 2013, both from the National University of Rosario (UNR, Argentina). From 2013 to 2015, I worked at the La Plata Astrophysics Institute (IALP), under a CONICET postdoctoral fellowship. In 2016, I was appointed as a full-time Researcher at IALP. Since 2024, I have been based at the Rosario Physics Institute (IFIR), where I work to this day.

My research focuses on stellar clusters, and the development of Python-based astrophysical software.
